READING THE MODEL

Sources & accuracy

Biology, not a biopsy

This model emphasizes compartment relationships, membrane topology and recognizable morphology. A real cell is far more crowded and changes shape continuously. Organelle proportions, orientations and abundances vary by cell type and state.

Geometry and metadata tests check declared illustration invariants. They are not independent scientific validation, and this model is not a molecular simulation or a clinically validated image.

What you are seeing

  • Interphase chromatin fibers, not condensed mitotic chromosomes.
  • Paired nuclear membranes with stylized eightfold pore rings; double mitochondrial membranes with exposed cristae.
  • ER surface-bound ribosomes; no ribosomes in Golgi cisternae.
  • Open display cutaways and exaggerated membrane spacing. No exact scale bar.
  • A mammalian cell: no cell wall, chloroplasts or central plant vacuole.
