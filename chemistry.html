Sources, coverage & model limits

Compound identity and formula facts: NIH PubChem, with individual CID links on each match. Atom counts and charges are validated locally. This finite catalog is not all possible chemistry. An absent match is not evidence that no substance exists.

Balanced-reaction facts and teaching geometry: OpenStax Chemistry 2e, chapter 4, §7.6 molecular geometry, and §10.6 ionic lattices. Each reaction links to its source. Shapes are illustrative ideals, not measured coordinates.

Only water, carbon dioxide, methane and ammonia have grounded molecular layouts here. Sodium chloride uses an alternating-ion lattice fragment, not a discrete NaCl molecule. Other compounds and unresolved isomers are composition-only views with no asserted bonds. Reaction animations are atom bookkeeping: paths, groups and timing are not mechanisms, molecular structures or literal particle counts.

PubChem aggregates source-specific rights; no blanket license is claimed for third-party records. OpenStax source snapshots and excerpts retain the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms identified in the retrieved pages. Access for free at Chemistry 2e, OpenStax / Rice University. We use attributed factual formula/equation subsets and original explanatory text and artwork, not copied textbook illustrations. Reproducible retrieval, snapshots and hashes are documented in CHEMISTRY-SOURCES.md. Optional network search sends only the selected formula to NIH PubChem; no account, key, backend, or persistent storage.
