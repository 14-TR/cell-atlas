Sources, data reuse & model limits

Names, symbols, families and neutral-atom configurations: NIH PubChem periodic table. Lawrencium uses the NIST ASD 7p ground configuration rather than the older 6d entry. Shell totals are sums by principal quantum number, including transition-element exceptions, not a sequential 2n² fill.

Representative isotopes: NIST isotopic compositions, with Ts-294 from the IUPAC 2022 table. Some listed isotope masses and superheavy configurations are estimated or predicted. One isotope does not describe every atom of an element.

Factual subset retrieved 4 October 2026 (UTC). Original sources retain their rights: PubChem aggregates source-specific terms, including LANL copyright terms; NIST data / SRD terms distinguish public-domain government works from copyrighted compilations. No source artwork is used and no blanket Creative Commons license is claimed for third-party data. Attribution and reproducible extraction are documented in the repository’s DATA-SOURCES.md.

These are animated teaching models, not quantum or nuclear-dynamics simulations. Moving electron markers follow illustrative shell guides, not literal electron trajectories or observed motion. The optional cloud is a qualitative orbital-inspired illustration, not a solved multielectron wavefunction or quantitative probability map. Particle sizes, distances, speeds and nucleus packing are not to scale. Colors are illustrative. No chemical bonding, ions or radioactive decay are simulated.
